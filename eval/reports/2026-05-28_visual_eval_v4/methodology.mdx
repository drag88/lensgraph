5. Top-5 MaxSim chunks come from the same video but transcript-text is unrelated to the question (e.g. "buy more H100s", "looking through your recent publications"). MaxSim is preferring whole-frame visual similarity over slide-text relevance.

## Stop-condition resolution — defensible failure with the blocker named

**Two stacked blockers: a pooled-prefilter recall miss at production K, and a ColQwen2.5 MaxSim/ranking failure underneath it.** Not OCR, not source resolution.

Evidence:
- At production `prefilter_k=200`, 7/10 gold frames miss the prefilter entirely — the channel is prefilter-limited before MaxSim even runs.
- Prefilter rescue at K=500 is complete (10/10 frames reach stage 2) but pass@5 stays 0/10 — so widening prefilter is necessary but not sufficient.
- MaxSim gaps to top-5 cutoff are small (+1.0 to +2.5 units) AND consistent across K — the model genuinely scores other frames marginally higher.
- 720p source resolution did not improve grounding even though it should be the highest-quality input ColQwen can consume (the processor band downsamples 720p to ~1034×581 which is still 2.5× the 360p area).
- OCR was untested in any positive case — no frame ever reached the OCR gate.
- The text-side 4-channel RRF still passes 18/18 on span overlap. The eval-as-product story does not break; visual retrieval is the part that doesn't currently help.

The fix has two parts: widen the prefilter so gold frames survive stage 1, AND fix ranking over the wider candidate set. Options for the next phase (the week-8 research matrix in `dev/active/phase-2-week-8/research_matrix.md` evaluates these against current models with links/dates):

1. **Widen `prefilter_k` to 500** as the floor for any further work — without it, 7/10 rows cannot pass regardless of the ranking fix. Cheap config change; first move in any experiment.
2. **Replace MaxSim with a different aggregator OR add a reranker over the candidate chunks.** Options on the existing ColQwen patches: top-K mean instead of sum, IDF-weighted sum, or a reranker. Any reranker must cover **at least the top-200 candidate chunks** (the gold chunks rank 137-172 after MaxSim, so a top-100 rerank would miss them), or rerank the frame candidates before the chunk truncation. `BAAI/bge-reranker-v2-m3` (already in the bakeoff #1 stack) can score over OCR'd frame text. Lowest-cost path; no new model.
3. **Add a second visual_retrieval candidate** (requires an ADR 004 amendment). Candidates surfaced in the research matrix: a different ColPali-family model, a slide-tuned single-vector embedding model for the prefilter, or a hosted multimodal embedding baseline. Pricing + integration work.
4. **Hybrid frame→OCR→text-rerank or frame→VLM-judge** over the top-20 candidate frames. Keeps visual retrieval as the candidate generator and adds a text/VLM ranking signal on top.

Do NOT retire visual retrieval and do NOT switch to OCR-only — both were explicitly ruled out for this phase. Recommendation: the smallest reversible diagnostic is option (1) + a slice of (2)/(4) measured side by side; the research matrix names the exact experiment.

## What this report does NOT establish

- Whether real-slide OCR works. Untested for the same reason as v3 — no frame ever reached the OCR gate, even at K=500 in the production pipeline. The OCR backend (pytesseract + Tesseract 5.x) is installed and mocked tests pass; spot-checking on the curated `.jpg` evidence frames is still the cheapest sanity check.
- Why ColQwen produces NaN on specific 720p inputs. 15 frames skipped this run; root cause not investigated. Repro: `frames/nXafozNIk3c/frame_000021.png` is one of them.
- Whether bge-reranker-v2-m3 would rescue any row when given the top-200+ MaxSim candidates (the gold chunks rank 137-172, so the rerank window must be ≥200). Listed as option (2) above.
