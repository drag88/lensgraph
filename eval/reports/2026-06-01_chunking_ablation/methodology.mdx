**Outcome:** ⚠️ **PROVISIONAL — no winner.** Retrieval/substrate comparison only; boundary edge/standalone NOT scored (boundary judge has only a provisional edge-kappa). The headline finding is about the **gold spans**, not the strategies.

## Setup

Both strategies coexist in `chunks` (natural key includes `chunking_strategy`);
retrieval is strategy-filtered (every channel takes `chunking_strategy`, default
`fixed_window` so production is unaffected). Same 10 single_clip dev_gold
queries, same channels, top-5. transcript_segment chunked+embedded via the
strategy-aware `chunk_handler` + drain.

## The headline: the gold spans are coarse (avg 108s)

dev_gold single_clip spans average **108.3s** (min 62, max 155). That one fact
explains every number above:

- **TR@5 = 1.00 is trivially easy** — a 108s answer region overlaps many chunks
  at any granularity. It was never going to discriminate.
- **GoldSpanContained@5 = 0.00 for both** — no chunk *fully wraps* a 108s span.
  fixed_window's 30s chunks are far too small; transcript_segment's 50–150s
  chunks are closer but **non-overlapping, so they split the span at a sentence
  boundary** and still don't contain it.
- **IoU@1 is low for both (0.15–0.26)** because the gold span (108s) and the
  chunks (30–150s) are mismatched in size and offset. transcript_segment's
  larger, span-scale chunks align *slightly* better on the fused ranking
  (0.23 vs 0.15) — a mild, not decisive, point in its favor *for this gold set*.

**So the bottleneck is not the chunking strategy — it's that the gold spans are
100s-scale answer *regions*, not tight clips.** No chunk granularity localizes a
108s span tightly. Until that is resolved, IoU/Contained cannot crown a chunking
winner; they mostly measure span-vs-chunk size mismatch.

## Decision

**No winner. Provisional.** The added metrics did their job: they exposed that
TR@5 = 1.00/1.00 was hiding poor localization (Contained 0.00, IoU ~0.2) driven
by coarse gold spans. No `lock_bakeoff_winner`, no ADR amendment.

**Next, in order:**
1. **Review the dev_gold spans.** Decide whether a "clip" is the full ~108s
   answer region (then chunk-level IoU is the wrong yardstick and we report
   region-recall) or a tighter ~20–40s moment (then re-curate spans, and
   IoU/Contained become meaningful chunking discriminators).
2. Only then re-run IoU/Contained as the chunking selection metric.
3. Add the corpus-fit strategies (slide_boundary; a cheap embedding-similarity
   semantic chunker) once the metric discriminates.
4. Citation accuracy + cold-validated boundary kappa for the answer/boundary tiers.
