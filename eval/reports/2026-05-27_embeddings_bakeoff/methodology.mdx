## TL;DR

BGE-M3 cleared the embeddings minimum (`timestamp_recall_at_5_vector_only_min` = 0.75) on the first measurement against `eval/corpora/ai_engineering_v0/dev_gold.jsonl`. Both the dense and multi-vector channels returned perfect TR@5 across all 10 single_clip examples; sparse alone fell to 0.70 (below the per-channel minimum) but the rule is "best of vector channels" and the dense + multivec channels carry it well over. RRF fusion across BM25 + the three BGE-M3 channels landed at 0.90 — one example (`sally-staying-on-task-attention`) where BM25 noise dragged the fused top-5 off the gold span even though dense and multivec individually nailed it. Worth a note for chunking ablation week; not a blocker here.

The one candidate with a complete substrate today is `bge-m3-all-channels`. Voyage and Gemini Embedding 2 are fallbacks under ADR 004 v3.1, gated on BGE-M3 failing the minimum. They did not fail, so no ADR amendment is required and no API spend was triggered.

## TR@5 definition

For an example with gold span `[s, e]` on `video_id = V`, a channel `passes@5` if any top-5 retrieved chunk satisfies:

```
chunk.video_id == V
AND chunk.start_sec < e
AND chunk.end_sec   > s          # open-interval overlap, not full containment
```

`TR@5 = passes / total`. The "vector channel only" minimum in ADR 004 v3.1 is interpreted as `max(dense, sparse, multivec)` — the best-of vector channels, not their mean. RRF is reported but blends BM25 and therefore does not count toward the vector-only minimum.

## What this measurement does NOT tell us

- **Generator/judge faithfulness.** Bakeoff #2 (week 7) runs `ClaimsSupported`, `CitationAccuracy`, and judge–human Cohen's kappa. Embeddings recall is necessary but not sufficient for end-to-end faithfulness.
- **Reranker lift.** Bakeoff #1 measures pre-rerank channels only. `bge-reranker-v2-m3` is in the loop in production (`retrieve/rerank.py`) but its `Recall@3` lift is its own minimum (`recall_at_3_lift_pp_min: 5.0`) and is measured separately when the reranker bakeoff lands.
- **Visual channel.** No `.mp4` files staged, so `frames` / `frame_patches` are empty. The visual channel returns `[]` gracefully and is excluded from the text-only embeddings minimum by ADR 004 v3.1.
- **Behavior at v1 corpus scale.** TR@5 = 1.0 on 10 examples is comfortable headroom but the per-channel gap between sparse (0.70) and dense (1.00) suggests sparse may be the first to break at larger corpus / harder questions. Worth watching in week-8 chunking ablation.
