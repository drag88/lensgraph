## TL;DR

ColQwen2.5 visual retrieval ran end-to-end against 8 verified visual_gold examples on 529 ingested frames (~310 patches each) across 3 talks. **Standalone visual recall is weak on this corpus (1/8 chunk TR@5, 1/8 frame recall@5).** **Visual lift over the 4-channel text stack is 0 pp** — but that result is methodologically pinned: the 4-channel text RRF already passes 8/8 on these examples (text channels saturate), so this corpus cannot measure whether visual fusion helps when text fails. Bakeoff #1 already showed text channels are excellent here (vector-best TR@5 = 1.00 on dev_gold).

No visual quality gate is encoded in ADR 004 v3.1, so this is **not a lock-or-fail decision** — it is a quality measurement that informs whether ColQwen is earning its keep in the production fusion. Today's signal is "doesn't help on text-saturated examples; on its own, weaker than expected; insufficient evidence to remove it" — needs visual-required-only curation in v1 before a definitive call.

## What the two passes tell us

**`tengyu-retrieval-accuracy-plot` — chunk passed, frame did not.** The pooled-vector prefilter (stage 1) put no in-span frame in its top 5, but the 3-stage MaxSim refine over the candidate chunks pulled in chunks 16 (374.96–404.64) and 17 (399.92–431.67) at ranks 4 and 1 respectively — both overlap the gold span [305, 410]. This validates the design intent of the 3-stage pipeline: **MaxSim over patches inside a wider candidate set can surface a chunk that the pooled-vector pre-rank missed.** It also says the pooled-mean is a coarse signal on its own for this query distribution.

**`sally-large-json-composable-tools` — frame passed, chunk did not.** Three frames inside the gold span ranked top-1, top-2, top-3 by pooled cosine (frame_sec 480, 490, 540 in [460, 615]). But the 3-stage chunk path put no overlapping chunk in its top 5. Possible: those frames mapped via the half-open `[chunk.start, chunk.end)` interval to chunks that ARE in the gold span, but MaxSim then re-ranked OTHER chunks higher (a chunk with more candidate patches contributed by the prefilter outranks one with the gold-span frames). Worth digging into when v1 visual gold is bigger — this is the kind of edge that the chunking ablation needs to surface.

## What the lift says

`with_visual_pass_rate = 1.00`, `without_visual_pass_rate = 1.00`, **`lift = 0.0 pp`**. The 4-channel text RRF already retrieves the gold chunk in its top 5 on every example, so adding the visual channel as a 5th cannot change the pass rate. **This corpus cannot answer "does visual fusion help?"** — by construction, text retrieval is saturated. The honest framing in the writeup: "visual retrieval does no harm to the production stack on these examples; whether it helps in cases where text fails is a separate question requiring visual-required-only gold examples."

## What this measurement does NOT decide

- **ColQwen is not removed from the production loop on this evidence.** 8 examples is small-N. Removing the visual channel based on "lift = 0 on a text-saturated set" is a methodology mistake — the lift was measured under conditions where it could not show.
- **No model swap is justified.** ADR 004 v3.1 has no visual minimum encoded; this run does not propose adding one. Adding a minimum would require a curated "visual-required" gold set first.
- **No "ColQwen is broken" conclusion.** 1/8 on standalone recall on natural-language questions against slide images is below expectation, but the failure mode (pooled prefilter misses, MaxSim sometimes catches) is consistent with the ColQwen2.5 design and not an obvious bug.

## What would make the next visual eval more informative

1. **Curate visual-required gold.** Questions where the answer is on the slide and NOT in the transcript (e.g. specific numbers / labels visible in a diagram that the speaker does not quote verbatim). On a visual-required gold set, `without_visual` would drop below 1.0 and `lift` would have measurable signal.
2. **Tune resolution / encoder config.** ADR 005 flagged the ColQwen processor `min_pixels` / `max_pixels` band as unverified. A 720p capture might be downsampled past slide-text legibility. Print the actual band and verify against the captured PNG dimensions before drawing strong conclusions from these numbers.
3. **Per-channel rank inspection.** When chunk_pass is False, was the gold chunk at rank 6? rank 50? The current eval records the top-5 only. A richer trace (top-20) would distinguish "near miss" from "not found at all" without re-running the whole pipeline.

## Metrics (definitions, unchanged from the STUB report)

### VisualFrameRecall@k
Pass if any of the top-k frames from `retrieve.visual.retrieve_frames` satisfies `frame.video_id == gold.video_id AND gold.start_sec - tolerance_sec <= frame.frame_sec <= gold.end_sec + tolerance_sec`. Default `tolerance_sec = FRAME_SAMPLE_EVERY_SEC = 10.0`.

### VisualChunkTR@k
Pass if any of the top-k chunks from `retrieve.visual.retrieve` overlaps the gold span (open-interval — `chunk.end_sec > gold.start_sec AND chunk.start_sec < gold.end_sec`).

### VisualLift@k
Per-example pass on `rrf({bm25, dense, sparse, multivec, visual}, top_k=5)` vs `rrf({bm25, dense, sparse, multivec}, top_k=5)`, reported as `(with_rate - without_rate) * 100` in percentage points.
