## What changed

Before this run, a positive grounding row was a single `true` in
`metrics.visual_answer_grounding_at_k`. There was nothing to spot-check: which
frame was OCR'd, which slide text it found, which curator term hit — none of it
was recorded. The 2026-05-28 run scored 0/10, so the gap was harmless then. The
fixed-loader run scored 2/10, and a positive boolean with no evidence is not a
methodology claim.

The scalar is now replaced by a JSONB sub-object,
`metrics.visual_answer_grounding` (dict or `null`), assembled in
`measure_visual.frame_ocr_grounding_at_k` and persisted by
`run_visual_eval._write_per_example_results`:

- `passed` — bool. The whole sub-object is `null` for a not-evaluable row (no
  curator evidence), matching how `answer_term_hit_at_k` carries `null`.
- `evaluated_frame_ids` / `evaluated_image_paths` — the in-window top-k frames
  the OCR judge actually ran against (right `video_id`, `frame_sec` inside the
  gold span ± 10s), index-aligned.
- `ocr_excerpts` — first 500 chars of OCR per evaluated frame, keyed by frame_id.
- `matched_term` — the curator term(s) that grounded the pass (joined with
  ` AND ` across a multi-item conjunction), or `null` on a fail.
- `failure_reason` — `no_in_window_frame` | `ocr_no_terms_matched` |
  `row_not_evaluable` (the `null`-sub-object label) | `null` on pass.
- `judge_kind` — `ocr` today; the field reserves the future VLM-judge path.

The run-level float stays in `eval_runs.summary.visual_answer_grounding_at_k`.
There is no back-compat shim — the per-row scalar key is gone.

- `matched_term`: `Sort LLM spans by latency AND todo_update(id=1, status="completed")`.
- The pass is driven by frame **995** (`frame_000035.png`, 340s).
- Hand check: the slide shows `0. [x] Sort LLM spans by latency` and
  `Call todo_update(id=1, status="completed")`. Confirmed — both terms are on
  the slide. **Survives.**

One honest caveat worth recording. With `normalize: exact`, item 2 requires a
straight double quote. Tesseract read the closing quote as a curly `”` on frames
992/993/994 and as a straight `"` only on 995, so the conjunction passed on a
single frame out of four near-identical slide captures. The slide genuinely
renders a straight quote (monospace code), so this is an OCR-fidelity artifact on
the three misses, not a false positive on 995 — but it shows the exact-match OCR
gate is one glyph away from flipping. If the grounding rate is ever published as a
claim, switch this row's quote-bearing term to a `lowercase_collapse_ws` normalize
or a quote-insensitive variant, or note the fragility next to the number.

## Why AnswerTermHit is 0/10 while grounding is 2/10

The two metrics read different text. AnswerTermHit checks the retrieved chunk's
*transcript* text; grounding checks the retrieved frame's *OCR* text. The
curator terms here are slide-only strings (code identifiers, plan checkboxes,
camelCase names) that the speaker never reads aloud verbatim, so they appear in
OCR but not in the transcript. That AnswerTermHit is 0 and grounding is 2 is the
expected signature of slide-bound answers, not a contradiction.
